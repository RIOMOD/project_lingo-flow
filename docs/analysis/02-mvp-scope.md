# 2. Pham vi MVP

## 2.1 Muc tieu MVP

MVP can du de van hanh mot he thong hoc tieng Anh co the thu hut nguoi hoc, cho phep Teacher tao noi dung, Admin duyet va quan ly duoc viec mua khoa hoc.

## 2.2 Bao gom trong MVP

| Hang muc | Noi dung |
|---|---|
| Xac thuc va phan quyen | Dang ky, dang nhap, role Student, Teacher, Admin |
| Catalog khoa hoc | Xem danh sach, chi tiet, gia, giao vien, curriculum, preview |
| Khoa hoc FREE | Student dang ky va hoc ngay |
| Khoa hoc PAID | Gio hang, coupon, checkout, thanh toan, cap quyen hoc |
| Quan ly noi dung | Teacher tao khoa hoc, chuong, bai hoc, vocab, grammar, bai tap, test |
| Phe duyet | Admin duyet noi dung, duyet gia, xuat ban, an khoa hoc |
| Hoc tap | Hoc bai, lam bai tap, lam bai kiem tra, xem tien do |
| AI co ban | Chatbot text va AI sua bai viet |
| Bao cao co ban | Lich su mua, doanh thu, thong ke hoc vien va giao dich |

## 2.3 Chua dua vao MVP

| Hang muc | Ly do de sau |
|---|---|
| Mobile app native | Uu tien web de ra san pham nhanh |
| Livestream va lop hoc truc tiep | Phuc tap van hanh va ha tang |
| Cham speaking bang AI nang cao | Ton kem va phuc tap hon chatbot text |
| Subscription hang thang | Chua can cho giai doan dau |
| He thong affiliate | Khong phai nhu cau cot loi |
| Gamification sau | Co the them sau khi co du lieu hanh vi |

## 2.4 Nguyen tac cat pham vi MVP

- Uu tien luong nghiep vu tao gia tri som nhat.
- Uu tien phan he nao can de tao doanh thu.
- Loai bo chuc nang phuc tap nhung khong anh huong den viec hoc va mua khoa hoc.
- Bao dam cac quy trinh nhay cam nhu payment, duyet khoa hoc va cap quyen hoc phai hoan chinh ngay tu MVP.
