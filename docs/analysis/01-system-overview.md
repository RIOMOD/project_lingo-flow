# 1. Tong quan he thong

## 1.1 Ten du an

He thong ho tro hoc tieng Anh thong minh.

## 1.2 Muc tieu kinh doanh

- Xay dung nen tang hoc tieng Anh truc tuyen cho ca khoa hoc mien phi va tra phi.
- Ho tro day du ky nang hoc tu vung, ngu phap, nghe, noi, doc va viet.
- Tich hop AI de tang tinh ca nhan hoa va kha nang tu luyen.
- Tao mo hinh van hanh co kiem soat giua Student, Teacher va Admin.
- Tich hop quy trinh thuong mai dien tu dung cho san pham so.

## 1.3 Nhom nguoi dung chinh

- `Guest`: Tim hieu khoa hoc, hoc thu, dang ky, dang nhap.
- `Student`: Hoc, mua khoa hoc, theo doi tien do, dung AI.
- `Teacher`: Tao noi dung, gui duyet, theo doi hoc vien va doanh thu khoa cua minh.
- `Admin`: Quan tri he thong, duyet khoa hoc, duyet gia, quan ly giao dich va bao cao.

## 1.4 Gia tri cot loi cua he thong

- Tap trung hoa noi dung hoc tieng Anh.
- Ho tro hieu qua tu hoc qua theo doi tien do va bai tap.
- Ket hop AI chatbot hoi thoai va AI sua bai viet.
- Kiem soat chat luong noi dung va gia ban qua luong phe duyet.
- Dam bao thanh toan dung, cap quyen hoc dung va co the kiem toan.

## 1.5 Phan he chinh

- `Identity and Access`: Dang ky, dang nhap, phan quyen, khoa mo tai khoan.
- `Course Catalog`: Danh sach khoa hoc, chi tiet, loc, tim kiem, hoc thu.
- `Course Authoring`: Tao khoa hoc, chuong, bai hoc, vocab, grammar, bai tap, bai kiem tra.
- `Learning`: Hoc bai, nop bai, lam test, luu tien do, xem diem.
- `Commerce`: Gio hang, coupon, don hang, giao dich, webhook, hoan tien.
- `AI Support`: Chatbot hoi thoai va AI sua bai viet.
- `Reporting and Admin`: Bao cao, doanh thu, quan tri tai khoan, audit log.

## 1.6 Kien truc muc cao de xuat

- `Frontend Web`: Giao dien cho Guest, Student, Teacher, Admin.
- `Backend API`: Xu ly nghiep vu, phan quyen, validation, tinh gia, cap quyen hoc.
- `Database`: Luu user, khoa hoc, don hang, giao dich, tien do, bai nop.
- `Object Storage`: Luu audio, video, tai lieu va tep bai hoc.
- `Payment Gateway`: Xu ly thanh toan va webhook.
- `AI Service`: Dich vu chatbot va sua bai viet.
- `Logging and Monitoring`: Theo doi loi, giao dich va su kien nhay cam.

## 1.7 Thuc the du lieu cot loi

- `User`, `Role`, `TeacherProfile`, `StudentProfile`
- `Course`, `Chapter`, `Lesson`, `Vocabulary`, `GrammarTopic`
- `QuestionBank`, `Exercise`, `Test`, `Submission`
- `Cart`, `Coupon`, `Order`, `OrderItem`, `PaymentTransaction`, `Refund`
- `Enrollment`, `LearningProgress`, `ScoreRecord`
- `AIConversation`, `WritingCorrection`, `AuditLog`
