# 8. Luong nghiep vu chinh

## 8.1 Luong Guest

1. Guest vao trang chu.
2. Guest xem danh sach khoa hoc da xuat ban.
3. Guest loc va xem chi tiet khoa hoc.
4. Guest hoc thu bai co `isPreview=true`.
5. Guest dang ky hoac dang nhap khi muon hoc day du.

## 8.2 Luong Student

1. Student dang nhap.
2. Student cap nhat ho so neu can.
3. Student tim khoa hoc phu hop.
4. Student dang ky khoa FREE hoac mua khoa PAID.
5. Student hoc bai, lam bai tap, lam test.
6. Student dung chatbot AI va AI sua bai viet.
7. He thong cap nhat tien do, diem va lich su mua.

## 8.3 Luong Teacher

1. Teacher dang nhap.
2. Teacher tao khoa hoc nhap.
3. Teacher them chuong, bai hoc, vocab, grammar, bai tap, test.
4. Teacher chon bai preview va de xuat gia.
5. Teacher gui khoa hoc cho Admin duyet.
6. Teacher theo doi hoc vien, diem, tien do, luot mua va doanh thu khoa cua minh.

## 8.4 Luong Admin

1. Admin quan ly user va Teacher.
2. Admin xem khoa hoc cho duyet.
3. Admin duyet noi dung va duyet gia.
4. Admin xuat ban hoac an khoa hoc.
5. Admin quan ly don hang, giao dich, coupon, refund.
6. Admin theo doi doanh thu va hoat dong he thong.

## 8.5 Luong mua khoa hoc PAID

1. Student chon khoa hoc PAID.
2. He thong kiem tra khoa hoc chua so huu va khong nam trong gio.
3. Student them khoa hoc vao gio.
4. Student ap coupon neu co.
5. Backend tinh lai gia, giam gia va tong tien.
6. Student tao don hang va chuyen qua cổng thanh toan.
7. Gateway gui webhook.
8. He thong xac minh webhook, cap nhat order va payment.
9. Neu thanh cong thi tao enrollment.

## 8.6 Luong dang ky khoa hoc FREE

1. Student dang nhap.
2. Student chon khoa hoc FREE.
3. He thong kiem tra chua so huu.
4. He thong tao enrollment ngay lap tuc.
5. Student hoc duoc toan bo noi dung khoa hoc.

## 8.7 Luong hoc thu

1. Guest hoac Student chua so huu vao chi tiet khoa hoc.
2. He thong chi cho truy cap bai co `isPreview=true`.
3. Bai khong phai preview se bi chan.
4. He thong goi y dang ky hoac mua khoa hoc.

## 8.8 Luong duyet khoa hoc

1. Teacher gui khoa hoc.
2. Khoa hoc chuyen sang `SUBMITTED`.
3. Admin xem noi dung va gia.
4. Admin approve hoac reject kem ly do.
5. Teacher sua va gui lai neu bi reject.
6. Khoa hoc da duyet moi duoc xuat ban.

## 8.9 Luong thanh toan thanh cong

1. Gateway gui webhook thanh cong.
2. He thong xac minh chu ky.
3. He thong kiem tra giao dich da xu ly chua.
4. He thong cap nhat `Payment SUCCESS` va `Order PAID`.
5. He thong tao `Enrollment ACTIVE`.
6. Student nhan thong bao mua thanh cong.

## 8.10 Luong thanh toan that bai

1. Gateway bao giao dich that bai hoac bi huy.
2. He thong cap nhat `Payment FAILED` hoac `Order CANCELED`.
3. He thong khong tao enrollment.
4. Student co the thanh toan lai bang lan moi.

## 8.11 Luong hoan tien

1. Student gui yeu cau hoan tien hoac Admin tu xu ly theo chinh sach.
2. Admin kiem tra dieu kien hoan tien.
3. Neu hop le, Admin thuc hien refund.
4. Khi refund thanh cong, he thong cap nhat giao dich va don hang.
5. He thong thu hoi quyen hoc neu chinh sach yeu cau.
6. He thong ghi audit log.
